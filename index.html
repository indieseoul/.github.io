PODWAVE / PODWAVE PLUS

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Podwave and Podwave Plus are provided by indieseoul (the “developer”). This policy explains how information is handled when you browse, search for, stream and download podcasts using these apps.

Privacy questions and requests: indieseoul@gmail.com.

At a glance
  • No Podwave account is required. Your library and listening progress are stored on your device.
  • Search and playback connect directly to podcast-directory and publisher services.
  • Podwave uses Google AdMob for app advertising. Podwave Plus does not contain an app advertising SDK.
  • Publisher advertisements within podcast audio can appear in either edition.

1. Apps covered

This policy covers the ad-supported Podwave app (com.podwave.app.free) and the paid Podwave Plus app (com.podwave.app.paid). Each edition keeps its own local data. Purchasing Plus does not automatically transfer your subscriptions or downloads from another edition.

2. Information on your device

The apps store subscriptions, recent searches and cached results, listening history and playback positions, queue items, playback settings, downloaded episodes and cached artwork on your device. These records support the library, search, resume-playback and offline-listening features.

The apps do not include a developer-operated account system, cloud-sync service, analytics service or crash-reporting backend. Android automatic app backup is disabled for these apps.

If you choose to export a subscription backup, the app writes a JSON file to the location you select. That location may be managed by a cloud-storage provider. Its own policies apply to files you save there. Subscription backup files do not contain downloaded audio or transfer listening history.

3. Podcast searches, artwork and audio

Popular-podcast and search requests are sent to Apple's public podcast directory. Search requests include the terms you enter. Opening a show, displaying artwork, streaming and downloading episodes send requests to podcast publishers, hosting providers and content-delivery networks.

These services receive the requested URL or content, your IP address and normal network-request information. They may keep access logs, measure listening or deliver publisher advertisements under their own policies. The developer does not control their independent processing or retention.

See Apple's privacy policy. For a particular podcast, consult the publisher's website and its hosting provider's privacy information.

4. Advertising in Podwave

The ad-supported edition integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares information including IP addresses, product interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers. This information is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses can be used to estimate general location. The app does not request precise device-location permission.

Ad handling depends on the applicable region, your choices and the configured advertising services. The app checks consent status and requests ads only when Google's consent platform reports that it may do so. Where required, consent messages are shown and you can revisit choices through Library → Ad privacy choices.

You can also review advertising controls in your Android and Google account settings. Rejecting personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all ads or the processing needed to deliver permitted ads.

Read Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising information for provider details.

5. Podwave Plus and payments

Podwave Plus does not include the Google Mobile Ads or User Messaging Platform SDKs and does not insert app banner ads. It still connects to podcast-directory and publisher services as described above.

Google Play handles payment for the paid app and associated purchase records. The app does not request or store your payment-card details. A purchase of Plus does not purchase access to paid podcast feeds or remove advertisements embedded by publishers in their episodes.

6. Permissions and security

Internet and network access allow directory searches and podcast delivery. Media-playback foreground-service and wake-lock permissions support user-started audio playback while the app is in the background. The advertising edition also includes advertising-identifier and related AdServices permissions supplied by its advertising SDK.

The apps do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. User-selected backup files are opened through Android's document picker.

Network requests use HTTPS where supported. The apps also permit legacy HTTP podcast feeds, images and media, so not every publisher request is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention and deleting information

Local information is retained until it is removed by the app, cleared by you, or removed through uninstalling. Artwork caches may be replaced as the app manages its cache.

  • Use the app's Downloads screen to remove downloaded episodes.
  • Use the search screen's clear-history control to clear recent searches.
  • Use Android Settings → Apps → Podwave or Podwave Plus → Storage → Clear data to erase the app's local state. Wording varies by device.
  • Delete exported subscription files separately from the folder or cloud service where you saved them.

There is no Podwave account to delete. Clearing local storage does not remove information independently retained by Apple, Google, podcast hosts or your chosen backup provider. Contact those providers or use their privacy controls for their records.

8. Support and privacy requests

If you contact the developer by email, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are used to respond to your request, troubleshoot the app and handle related privacy or security issues. Email-service providers process correspondence as part of delivering and storing email.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, handle related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information held by the developer by contacting the privacy email above. Information may need to be retained when required by law or to resolve an ongoing dispute.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact the developer to make a request. Verification may be needed before disclosing or deleting records. Requests concerning an independent provider's records should be directed to that provider.

9. Children and podcast content

These apps provide access to independently published podcasts, which may contain mature themes. They do not provide child-specific accounts or parental controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer through support correspondence, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed and information deleted where appropriate.

10. This webpage

This privacy page includes no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, cookies set by page code or embedded third-party media. The hosting provider may process technical information about visits under its own policies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

11. Policy changes

This page will be updated when the apps' information practices change. The effective date will identify the applicable version. Any additional notice or consent required for material changes will be provided as appropriate.
